Why Doctors Need This

Standard "Black Box" AI is not trusted in high-stakes medicine. Clinicians need to answer: "Why did the model say Pneumonia?" to trust the diagnosis.

Our Solution: Grad-CAM

We implemented Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping. It creates a heatmap overlay showing exactly which pixels influenced the model's final decision.

Result & Trust

  • Verifies the model looks at lungs, not equipment tags or bones.
  • Helps radiologists spot subtle consolidation they might miss.
