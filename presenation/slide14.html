Key Learnings & Insights

Technical Insights

  • Transfer Learning Works: Achieved +7% accuracy boost using pretrained ResNet18 compared to the baseline CNN.

  • Class Imbalance Matters: Implementing a weighted loss function was crucial to prevent bias towards the majority class.

  • Simple Models Can Work: Even a basic 3-layer CNN achieved 79% accuracy, serving as a strong baseline.

  • Explainability Builds Trust: Grad-CAM visualization is not optional; it is essential for clinical adoption.

Medical Insights

  • Safety First: High pneumonia recall (>95%) is non-negotiable to prevent missing dangerous cases.

  • Balance Matters: Reducing false alarms significantly improves the patient experience and reduces anxiety.

  • Visual Validation: Clinicians need to see the model's reasoning (heatmaps) to trust the diagnosis.

  • Incremental Improvement: Small gains in accuracy translate directly to better outcomes and saved lives.

Core Insight: Success requires balancing rigorous technical optimization with real-world clinical safety requirements.
