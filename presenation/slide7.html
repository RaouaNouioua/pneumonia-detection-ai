The Analogy

Transfer Learning

Our Implementation Workflow

1

Start with ResNet18

Load the model pre-trained on ImageNet. It already knows how to detect edges, textures, and complex shapes.

Pre-trained Weights
2

Freeze Layers

"Lock" all convolutional layers so their learned features aren't destroyed during training.

Parameters Frozen
3

Replace Classifier Head

Remove the final layer (1000 classes) and replace it with a new layer for our specific task.

1000 → 2 Classes
4

Train Final Layer

Train only the new classifier (1,026 parameters) to adapt the knowledge to Pneumonia detection.

Trainable
