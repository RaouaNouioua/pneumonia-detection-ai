ResNet18 Results — Qualitative Impact

Clinical Simulation

Projected impact analysis when screening a cohort of 1,000 patients in a hospital triage setting.

Before (Baseline CNN)

  • Only ~340 healthy patients correctly sent home.

  • ~660 False Alarms
    Majority of healthy patients flagged as sick.

  • Result: High anxiety & unnecessary testing for healthy individuals.

After (ResNet18)

  • ~670 correctly sent home
    Doubled the efficiency of clearing healthy cases.

  • ~330 False Alarms
    False positives reduced by 50%.

  • Safety Maintained: 974 pneumonia cases successfully detected.
